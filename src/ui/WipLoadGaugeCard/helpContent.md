# WIP Load

## Definition

Work in Progress (WIP) is the number of issues that have been started but not completed, including work that is blocked due to
handoffs or information constraints. WIP Load is the amount of work in progress relative to the number of people working.

## Goal of Measuring

**Keep WIP under control**. Excessive WIP leads to delayed value delivery. By stating more things than we are finishing, we are forced to context switch between tasks. this leads to delays while we reorient on what we are doing on each task. The lack of focus and flow also leads to lower quality and the delays result in reduced user satisfaction. Keeping WIP low is one of the core activities that improves the flow of value.

## How This is Measured

- **Really good**: The team is collaborating to get the most important item delivered as rapidly as possible (there is one issue and the whole team is working on that issue).
- **Good**: There are fewer items in progress than there are people on the team.
- **Fair**: There are more items in progress than there are people on the team.
- **Poor**: There are 1.5x more items in progress than there are people working on them.
- **Overloaded**: There are > 2x as as many items in progress as there are people working on them.

## Tips for Improvement

- Set WIP limits and do not exceed them.
- If work is blocked, focus on reviewing status on the blocked work daily and escallate for help if the blockage persists.
- Reduce the batch size of work. Small tasks deliver sooner and are less likely to be re-prioritized.

## Data Source

- Team size is calculated by the number of people who have worked on issues for the last 30 days
- WIP is the total number of items that are in progress that have been created within the last 6 months.

## Possible Data Quality Issues

- If the team is using a board that is not registered in the dashboard then that work will not be tracked.
- If the team is not moving issues to reflect the correct status then WIP will be inaccurate.
