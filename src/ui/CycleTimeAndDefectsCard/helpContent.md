# Cycle Time & Defects

## Definition

- [Cycle Time](https://dojoconsortium.org/metrics/development-cycle-time/). The average time between when work is started until it is delivered to production
- [Defects](https://dojoconsortium.org/metrics/quality/): The number of defects reported per week

## Goal of Measuring

- **Smaller tasks and stories**: Smaller tasks have fewer assumptions, more clarity, and deliver value faster with less waste.
- **Reduce toil**: Delivering more frequently helps standardize and automate delivery processes.
- **Improve the system of quality**: Tracking defect rates relative to delivery frequency helps improve quality and speed.

A good practice is for any planned work to take **less than 2 days** from started to delivered.
Driving down the batch size of work is a primary tool and leading indicator for improving the rest of the system of delivery. Tracking the size and frequency of batch delivery along with defect rates allows us to become more efficient and effective because the two metrics act as guardrails for each other.
