# Holocron Delivery Metrics

Holocron is designed to provide development teams, engineering leadership, and product stakeholders insights into the flow of product delivery. It is focused on metrics related to how we deliver work and the things that affect our ability to do that better. The ability to observe the system of delivery and identify issues that can impact future product goals is critical to maintaining the health of the product and the broader organization. Holocron is also designed to provide metrics-based information to help teams improve their workflow so that they can deliver value sooner, safer, and happier.

## Metrics Organization

Holocron follows a three-tier structure for metrics aggregation: organization, value stream, and team.

- Organization: The top-level aggregation of all team-level metrics to provide an overview of organizational health.
- Value Stream: A value stream can be thought of as a line of business or product line. This view shows only the aggregate metrics for the teams within that value stream.
- Team: The delivery metrics for a team with additional information needed for tracking the health of delivery pipelines. Additionally, it contains information to help teams archive a continuous integration workflow.

Each metric widget includes information on the meaning of the metric, what it indicates, how to use the metric to identify opportunities for improvement, and industry-tested methods for improvement.

## Data Sources

Holocron collects team workflow data and repository activity data to track key metrics for work in progress throughput, continuous integration, bach size, delivery frequency, defect rates, and many other metrics that are critical to identifying and improving the organization's flow of delivery.
