# Cumulative Flow

## Definition

Cumulative flow shows the relationship between work intake and work in progress.

## Goal of Measuring

**Level intake to output:** We want to have a sustainable balance of input and output. If the work on the backlog continuously grows then the organization is being over-utilized. This will result in growing lead times and pressure to increase WIP. This will result in degrading throughput and quality.

## Tips for Improvement

- Keep intake and output balanced.
- To increase throughput:
  - Find ways to reduce toil and wait times in the flow of delivery
  - Split and grow the team along architectural boundaries to horizontally scale delivery.

## Data Source

- Data is sourced from workflow tracking tools

## Possible Data Quality Issues

- Work is done but not tracked in the tool
